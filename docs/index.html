How I approach infrastructure

Automate safely

Prefer repeatable infrastructure, validation, policy gates, idempotent workflows and explicit rollback paths.

Design for failure

Use SLOs, observability, incident response, recovery planning and controlled failure experiments to reason about reliability.

Reduce cognitive load

Platform abstractions should provide golden paths while keeping ownership, security and operational trade-offs visible.
